The workshop hotel is the **Hampton Inn & Suites Madison / Downtown** at [440 W. Johnson St, Madison, WI 53703](http://maps.apple.com/?address=440+W+Johnson+St,Madison,WI+53703). The room rate is $164/night. 
Reservation may be made using the [**online registration form**](https://www.hilton.com/en/attend-my-event/msnbjhx-907-f67072a6-7699-4e58-a9ea-9d27fe6c9cf3/) or by calling the hotel at [**+1-608-255-0360**](tel:+1-608-255-0360) and ask for the *Scalable Tools Workshop 2026* room block. The quoted rate is also good for 3 days before and after the workshop.
